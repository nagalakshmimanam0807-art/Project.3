A note from the editors

The world is wide.
Stay curious.

Fieldnotes is an independent multimedia journal about the places, people, and ideas that make us look twice. We believe the best stories invite you to slow down, listen closely, and see the familiar in a new light.

Get to know us ↗
What is Fieldnotes?
An independent journal collecting thoughtful stories in words, images, and sound.
Who is it for?
Anyone with a curious mind and a few minutes to wander somewhere new.
How often do you publish?
Fresh stories arrive twice a month, with occasional notes from the road.

The Fieldnotes way

A little room
to roam.

No endless scroll, no noise. Just a thoughtful way to discover and keep the stories that stay with you.

  1. 01⌕

    Find a thread

    Browse by place, idea, or feeling. Follow the detail that catches your eye.

  2. 02◉

    Settle into a story

    Read, watch, or listen at your own pace. Every story is made to be savored.

  3. 03↗

    Carry it with you

    Take a new perspective into your day—and share it with someone curious.
